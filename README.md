# About the Agentic System

**BUS 4498 Team Build**

> **Problem to be solved**: Travelers often spend several hours planning a trip because they must repeatedly compare flights, hotels, transportation options, attractions, restaurant choices, and overall costs across multiple websites and apps. Plans can also become outdated when prices, availability, or personal preferences change. As a rough baseline, a traveler may spend 5–10 hours manually researching and organizing a single trip before finalizing an itinerary. The proposed system would reduce this planning burden by automatically gathering trip requirements, comparing available options, organizing them into an itinerary, tracking the budget, and recommending adjustments when conditions or preferences change.

TripSync Travel Planning Agent
### System Goal
The goal of the TripSync Travel Planning Agent is to reduce the time and effort required to plan a trip by automatically creating and updating a personalized travel itinerary based on the traveler’s destination, dates, budget, transportation needs, lodging preferences, interests, and scheduling constraints. The system will compare available options, organize selected activities into a realistic schedule, monitor the estimated total cost, identify conflicts or changes, and recommend adjustments that keep the trip aligned with the traveler’s preferences and budget.

### Who Is Better Off When This Works?

Travelers are better off when the system works because they can spend less time manually researching and coordinating trip details while receiving a more organized, personalized, and budget-conscious travel plan.
